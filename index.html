A shared direction

Building a stronger future, together

The Developmental and Epileptic Encephalopathies (DEE) Roundtable Report captures a shared vision for Rare and Complex Epilepsies in Australia. It brings together the voices, evidence and priorities of people and families with lived experience, clinicians, researchers, community organisations, industry and policy leaders.

The Roundtable Working Group

Working together for meaningful change

The DEE Roundtable brought together people and families with lived experience, clinicians, researchers, patient organisations, industry and policy perspectives.

The Working Group was established to carry the Roundtable’s shared direction forward: connecting the evidence, priorities and relationships needed to strengthen action for Rare and Complex Epilepsies in Australia.
